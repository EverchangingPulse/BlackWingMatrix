About this app & how to use it

Download this HTML file and open it directly in a modern browser. Everything runs on your device: no installation, server, network connection, external fonts, or libraries are needed. Use New puzzle for a fresh seed, or Load to reproduce a puzzle with its seed and settings. Changing a setting starts a new puzzle with the displayed seed.

BlackWingMatrix is a visual-pattern practice app. It generates original grid-based reasoning exercises, answers, scoring, hints, explanations, and an adaptive practice mode directly in your browser. Exercise complexity is not a standardized difficulty or IQ score.

Keyboard: 1–8 select an answer; Enter checks it; N starts a new puzzle when you are not editing a field. The Figure lab lets you explore the visual vocabulary independently of the puzzle generator.
